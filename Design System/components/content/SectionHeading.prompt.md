Opens a page section: eyebrow, serif title, gilt rule, optional one-sentence intro.

```jsx
<SectionHeading eyebrow="Die Karte" title="Was heute auf den Tisch kommt"
  intro="Wir kochen, was der Markt am Morgen hergibt." />
```

`align="center"` for full-width sections, left for grid columns. `tone="inverse"` inside pine bands.
