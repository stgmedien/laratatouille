A hairline linen panel for a story, event or press item. Default is `outline` — flat, no shadow.

```jsx
<Card href="#" imageSrc="../../assets/images/dish-placeholder.svg"
  eyebrow="Abende" title="Mittelmeer-Menü, 6 Gänge">
  Jeden Donnerstag, 19:30.
</Card>
```

`raised` only when a card floats over imagery. Hover (link cards only) lifts 2px and scales the cover 1.03 — no colour change.
