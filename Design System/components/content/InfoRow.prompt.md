Practical facts, one per row: hours, address, phone. The icon is always pine, the label always small caps.

```jsx
<InfoRow icon="clock" label="Öffnungszeiten">Mi – So, 19:00 – 23:30</InfoRow>
<InfoRow icon="map-pin" label="Adresse">Calle Mayor 14, 03769 Sanet y Negrals</InfoRow>
```

Stack 2–4 rows in a footer or contact column; use `tone="inverse"` on pine.
