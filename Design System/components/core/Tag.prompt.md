A pill label for dish and page attributes. The only pill-shaped thing in the system.

```jsx
<Tag tone="sage">Vegetarisch</Tag>
<Tag tone="gold">Signature</Tag>
```

Tones: `neutral` (hairline outline), `gold` (Signature — the one gold fill in the system), `sage` (vegetarian/seasonal), `pine`, `inverse` (on dark). Keep labels to one or two words, uppercase handled by the component.
